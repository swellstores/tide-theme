Tide is a complete storefront for any kind of shop, ready the moment you install it. It is simple and clean, on a white page with neutral colors and plain type, so it suits any store and is an easy starting point to make your own. Developers building themes for Wave start from it too.

**Every page, ready to go.** Home, category, product, search, cart, account and sign-in pages, a blog, content pages, policies and a "page not found" page all come laid out, plus About and Contact page layouts.

**A hero that works without photos.** Until you add an image, the home page opens with your store's name and a button on a clean white page. Add your own image when you are ready and choose a boxed, overlay, full-width or image-only layout, with the words left, centered or right and up to two buttons.

**Sections for your story.** A story with an image and pull quote, your own figures, customer quotes, a feature list, image tiles as a grid, mosaic or collage, and an FAQ. None of them invents copy: each stays hidden until you write it.

**A product page that sells.** Photos switch to the chosen variant. The buy box holds every option, stock states such as pre-order and sold out, and subscription plans with their savings and trial. Below it come the description, a bundle's contents, the product's attributes, up to four of your own promises (free returns, say), products that pair with it, and related products.

**Easy to find, quick to buy.** Category pages have filters, sort and a product count. Search suggests products as shoppers type. The cart opens in a drawer, takes coupon codes and can suggest add-ons beside the totals, and checkout is Swell's own.

**Accounts that do the work.** Customers see their orders with tracking and buy them again in one step, manage their addresses, and pause, resume or cancel subscriptions.

**Make it yours without code.** Set fonts, type size, spacing, page width, buttons and cards for the whole store in Wave's Theme tab, give any section its own colors, or ask the agent to recolor the whole store. The header can sit left or centered, or over a full-width hero image. The footer comes in columns, centered or as a large wordmark, with your payment icons.

**Edit it in Wave.** Click any section to change it, type over text on the page, and add, move or hide sections. Or ask Wave's AI agent in plain words; when Tide has no section for what you want, it writes one in Tide's style.

**Light for shoppers.** Tide's sections send no JavaScript of their own: filters, sort, add to cart and sign-in are plain links and forms that work before any script loads.

**Good to know.** Tide reads your products, categories, menus, contact details and policies from Swell, so set those up in your dashboard first. Swell has no contact form, so the Contact page shows your email, phone, address and opening hours instead. Star ratings appear only with a reviews app installed. Tide is built for the Wave editor and needs it installed.
